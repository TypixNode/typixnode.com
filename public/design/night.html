01 · Thermal design

The case is the cooler.

The compute module bonds thermally to the milled body, so 280 grams of aluminum do the work a fan would — silently, with no dust and nothing to wear out.

A CM4 holds sustained load at 0 dB. For CM5 heavy-lifting there's an optional 4-pin PWM header and a dedicated vented case, in development.

02 · Longevity

Upgrade the module.
Keep the machine.

The standard compute-module socket takes CM4 and CM5 directly, and the tiny CM0 through our castellated 132-pin adapter. When a faster module ships, your computer gets faster — the chassis stays.

Inside: M.2 NVMe, microSD, and a 40-pin GPIO header for hardware experiments.
