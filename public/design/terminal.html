Does it include a compute module?

Ships as a chassis kit. Add a CM4/CM5 at checkout or seat one you already own — it's the standard compute-module socket.

How does a fanless computer stay cool?

The CNC body is the heatsink. The module bonds thermally to the chassis; heat spreads across the whole body and leaves silently, even under sustained load.

Can I open and repair it?

Four captive screws, no glue, labeled connectors, public teardown guide. Spare parts and CAD are published, so repair never means replacement.

When does my pre-order ship?

Built in small batches; estimated dispatch end of Q3 2026. Tracking by email. Full refund any time before your unit ships.
