A pocket Linux computer, machined like a tool.

TypixDeck is a fanless Linux machine the size of your palm. The aluminum body is the heatsink, the shield and the structure — one part, milled from billet, holding a Raspberry Pi compute module you can swap in three minutes.

NOTE 1

Chassis = heatsink

The compute module is thermally bonded to the milled body. Heat spreads across 280 g of aluminum and leaves silently. No fan, no dust, no failure point.

NOTE 2

Four captive screws

No glue anywhere in the assembly. Connectors are labeled on silk. The teardown guide is printed and public — repair is a feature, not a violation of warranty.

NOTE 3

Standard CM socket

Fits Raspberry Pi CM4 and CM5; the tiny CM0 drops in through our castellated 132-pin adapter. The chassis outlives every module you'll ever seat in it.
