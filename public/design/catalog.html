Fig. 01 — TypixDeck

The chassis is the heatsink.

One billet of 6061-T6 aluminum, milled into a body that cools a Raspberry Pi compute module at zero decibels. No fan to fill with dust, no vent to whistle, nothing to wear out.

Four captive screws open it. The socket takes CM4, CM5 — or the tiny CM0 through our own 132-pin adapter — so the computer you buy this year is faster in three, with the same body.
