Frequently asked questions

Does it come with a Raspberry Pi compute module?

It ships as a chassis kit. Add a CM4 or CM5 at checkout, or drop in one you already own — it uses the standard compute-module socket.

How does a fanless computer stay cool?

The CNC aluminum chassis is the heatsink. The module is thermally bonded to the body, so heat spreads across the whole chassis and dissipates silently, even under sustained load.

Can I really open and repair it myself?

Yes. Four captive screws, no glue, labeled connectors, and a public teardown guide. Spare parts and CAD files are available.
